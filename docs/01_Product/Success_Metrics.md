# AutoQA AI — Success Metrics

**Version:** 0.1 Draft | **Status:** Targets require baseline

## North-star outcome

Percentage of approved requirements that reach an accepted, verified automation change with complete traceability and without unplanned manual repair.

## Product metrics

- median time from approved requirement to review-ready change;
- framework bootstrap and first-valid-test time;
- plan approval and generated-change acceptance rates;
- active projects completing the end-to-end workflow;
- repeat usage and design-partner retention.

## AI and quality metrics

- requirement extraction precision/recall;
- test-strategy expert score;
- generated patch compile/pass rate;
- bug-detection precision, recall and F1;
- false-positive and false-negative rates;
- mutation detection rate;
- failure-classification accuracy and confidence calibration;
- flaky-run rate and groundedness/citation rate.

## Operational metrics

Workflow success, queue time, run duration, cancellation success, artifact completeness, SLO attainment, model/tool latency, tokens and compute per accepted change.

## Guardrail metrics

Unauthorized-action attempts blocked, prompt-injection success rate, secret/PII leakage, safe-stop correctness, cross-tenant access failures and audit completeness.

Metrics require a definition, owner, data source, segmentation, baseline, target, alert threshold and known limitations before launch.
