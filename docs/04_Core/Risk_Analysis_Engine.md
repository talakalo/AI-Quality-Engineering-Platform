# AutoQA AI — Risk Analysis Engine

**Version:** 0.1 Draft

## Purpose

Prioritize verification based on business impact, likelihood, change surface, complexity, security, data integrity, integration criticality, observability and historical failures.

## Risk record

Scenario, affected users/data, cause, impact, likelihood, detectability, evidence, existing controls, proposed test layer, priority, owner and residual risk.

## Method

Use an explainable scoring rubric plus qualitative review. Scores rank discussion; they do not replace human judgment. Critical security and authorization risks cannot be averaged down.

## Outputs

Ranked risk register, coverage recommendations, excluded-risk warnings, required environments/data and approval needs.

## Metrics

Expert ranking agreement, escaped-defect relevance, high-risk coverage, calibration and reviewer override rate.
