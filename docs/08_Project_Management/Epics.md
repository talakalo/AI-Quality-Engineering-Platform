## Epic readiness

Owner, persona outcome, scope/out-of-scope, dependencies, measurable exit criteria, security/privacy impact, architecture decisions, evaluation/test plan and rollback are required before stories are marked ready.

## Epic completion

All accepted stories meet Definition of Done, end-to-end outcome is demonstrated, metrics are observable, documentation and runbooks are current, and residual risk is accepted by the correct owner.
